Grading Standards

Reference thresholds used for every quality classification.

AA Haugh Unit ≥ 72 Firm, high-quality — premium market grade
A Haugh Unit 60–71 Standard market quality
B Haugh Unit 31–59 Lower quality, limited retail use
C Haugh Unit < 31 Reject — not suitable for sale

What Each Measurement Means

Poids Weighed on a calibrated scale, in grams
Hauteur Albumen height, measured with a micrometer
Fraîcheur (HU) Calculated from weight and height via the Haugh formula
Charge de rupture Shell strength — force required to crack, in Newtons
